The way we work

Four steps. Same order, every time.

You do the choosing. We do the measuring, stitching, chasing and finishing.

  1. 01 · Enquire

    Call, WhatsApp or visit.

    Send a photo, or come see the fabric wall. Both routes end in the same shortlist.

  2. 02 · Free measurement

    We come to you.

    We visit, measure and note preferences. No obligation.

  3. 03 · Choose

    Fabric & finish.

    Shortlist brought to you, or picked from our wall. You pick from five, not from four thousand.

  4. 04 · Stitch & install

    Delivered and mounted.

    Cut, stitched, hung and squared by our team. Any drop off by a finger — we come back.

Why it hangs straight

Nothing is farmed out.

The measure, the cut, the stitch, the mount and the alteration all happen under one roof. When you call, you don't get transferred.

Every curtain, blind and cushion cover leaves through the same door it entered — reviewed by the same person from first shortlist to final install.
