## 02 · Carpets

**Title:** The floor becomes the room.

A carpet is the layer that finally makes a room feel like one — softer
underfoot in winter, quieter to walk through at midnight, kinder to a
toddler about to fall. Hand-knotted wool from Bhadohi for the rooms
that ought to last twenty years, flat-woven dhurries from Mirzapur for
the corridors that see daily traffic, and honest machine-mades when
the budget calls for one. Knot-count on every label — no inflated
numbers, no imported fiction.

- Hand-knotted wool
- Tibetan-knot silk
- Flat-weave dhurries
- Machine-mades — Belgium, Turkey

something firm. Motorised — and, more importantly, quiet — installed
by our own electrician, warranty through us.
