The way we work

Four steps. Same order, every time.

You do the choosing. We do the measuring, stitching, chasing and finishing.

  1. 01 · Enquire

    Call, WhatsApp or visit.

    Send a photo, or come see the fabric wall. Both routes end in the same shortlist — no wasted trips.

  2. 02 · Free measurement

    We come to you.

    We visit, measure and note preferences — window heights, ceiling drops, sofa dimensions. No obligation.

  3. 03 · Choose

    Fabric & finish.

    Shortlist brought to you, or picked from our wall. You pick from five, not from four thousand — decision fatigue removed.

  4. 04 · Stitch & install

    Delivered and mounted.

    Cut, stitched, hung and squared by our team. Any drop off by a finger — we come back the same week.

Why it hangs straight

Nothing is farmed out.

The measure, the cut, the stitch, the mount and the alteration all happen under one roof. When you call, you don't get transferred.

Every curtain, blind and cushion cover leaves through the same door it entered — reviewed by the same person from first shortlist to final install.

Names, not gambles

Every mattress, motor, blind and wallpaper we stock carries a factory warranty. We handle the claim on your behalf.
