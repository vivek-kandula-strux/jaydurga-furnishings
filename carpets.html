Choosing well

Read the knot, not the story.

A hand-knotted carpet is priced by the density of its knots, and the trade is full of inflated numbers. Ours carry the true knot-count on the label — turn a piece over on our floor and count for yourself. If you are not sure what a room needs, the consultation is free, and the swatches and samples are all on the collections page.

Wool for warmth and wear, silk where the light should catch it, cotton dhurries that shrug off the corridor. When the budget calls for a machine-made, we will say so — Belgium and Turkey, no imported fiction. Our price guide explains how the ranges compare before you visit.

Questions

Asked across the counter, answered here.

Will you measure the room before we buy?

Yes. The home visit and measurement are free — we measure the room, the passage or the staircase, and you get a firm quote before you commit to anything.

Can you make a runner to fit our hall or staircase?

Yes. Runners for halls, passages and staircases are cut to length and finished by our own in-house tailors, so the edging holds up to daily traffic.

Do you supply underlay and cleaning?

Both. We fit underlay beneath the carpet so it sits flat and wears evenly, and we run a cleaning service for the pieces we sell.

Is there a warranty on the carpets?

Warranty support runs through the shop — if something is wrong, you call us, not a call centre.
