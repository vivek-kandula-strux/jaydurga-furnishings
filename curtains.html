The range

For light, sleep, and everything you'd rather not hear.

Twenty-two sheer weights for the rooms that want daylight without the glare. Three-pass blackouts for the ones who like their weekends dark. Jacquards woven for us in Erode, printed cottons from Bhuj and Bagh for windows that want pattern. Every curtain is tailored by us, hung by us, and altered by us if the drop is a finger off.

Sheers and blackouts are often hung as a pair on a double track — the sheer for the day, the blackout for the night. Bring us the window sizes, or better, let us come and measure; the visit is free.

  • Sheers — voile, organza, leno
  • Blackouts — three-pass, motorised
  • Jacquards — Erode, Salem looms
  • Printed cotton — Bhuj & Bagh
  • Tracks & rods — brass, walnut
  • Hand-stitched headings

Fabric

Jacquard from Erode, cotton from Bhuj and Bagh.

The jacquards are woven for us in Erode, on Salem looms — weighty, patterned cloth that hangs in deep folds and keeps its shape for years. The printed cottons come from Bhuj and Bagh, block prints that take the sun well and wash without sulking. Sheers run from crisp voile to soft organza and open-weave leno.

Choosing is easier with the cloth in your hand. The bolts are on the floor in Ameerpet, and if you are not sure what suits the light in your room, the consultation is free — sit with the swatches and take your time.

Rods, tracks & motorised

How a curtain order runs.

  1. Measure

    We visit your home and measure every window — the visit and the measurement are free.

  2. Choose the fabric

    Sheers, blackouts, Erode jacquards or printed cotton — from the bolts on our floor, with a free consultation if you want one.

  3. Pick the hardware

    Brass or walnut rods, slim tracks, or a motorised track that opens the curtains for you.

  4. Stitch

    Our own tailors cut and stitch the curtains in-house, headings hand-stitched.

  5. Hang

    Our team fits the rod or track and hangs the curtains — and alters the drop if it is a finger off.

Questions

Asked across the counter, answered here.

Do you come home to measure the windows?

Yes. The home visit, measurement and consultation are free. We measure every window ourselves, so the finished curtain fits the drop exactly — and if it is a finger off, we alter it.

Who stitches the curtains?

Our own tailors, in-house. The same team that measures your windows cuts, stitches and hangs the curtains — including hand-stitched headings — so one team answers for the whole job.

Do you do motorised curtains?

Yes. We supply and fit motorised curtain tracks alongside the regular brass and walnut rods, and our own team installs them.

Is there a warranty on curtains and tracks?

Warranty support runs through the shop — if something is wrong with the stitching, the track or the fitting, you call us, not a call centre.
