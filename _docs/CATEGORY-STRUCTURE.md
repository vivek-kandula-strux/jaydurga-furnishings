# Proposed Category Structure — for your review

*Prepared alongside the revised homepage. Please approve or edit this taxonomy before we roll it out to the Collections page, footer and site navigation.*

---

## The change, in one line

We are collapsing the old eight-category grid (Curtains · Carpets · Mattresses · Blinds · Wallpapers · Upholstery · Bedding · Table linen) into **seven categories**, with **Curtains & Windows** leading and absorbing Blinds. This matches your instruction to position Jay Durga as a **home-furnishings house led by windows**, not a furniture-style listing.

---

## The seven categories (in the order they appear on site)

### 01 · Curtains & Windows  *(lead category)*
The homepage devotes an entire dark section to this alone. All window-related products live here.

**Sub-categories inside this tile:**
- Curtains & Drapes — full-drop, jacquards, prints, plains
- Sheers — soft-daylight panels
- Blackouts — bedroom / theatre grade
- Roman & Roller Blinds — chain or motorised
- Vertical & Venetian Blinds — fabric, PVC, aluminium
- Rods, Tracks & Motorised — straight rods, silent tracks, remote systems
- Valances, tie-backs, curtain accessories

### 02 · Sofa Fabrics & Cushioning
Replaces the old "Upholstery" tile and adds cushion fills. Covers sofa recovers, plain and printed fabrics, cushion inners and outers.

### 03 · Mattresses
Pocket-spring, latex, coir, memory foam. Delivered and set up.
*Brands stocked (to verify): Sleepwell, Kurlon, Duroflex, Peps.*

### 04 · Carpets & Rugs
Expanded from the old "Carpets" tile. Hand-knotted, machine-made, dhurries, runners.
*Sources (to verify): Bhadohi Weavers, Mirzapur Cooperatives, Bhuj Handloom.*

### 05 · Bedding
Sheets, quilts, comforters, pillows. Cotton and blends.

### 06 · Bath *(new)*
Towels, bath mats, shower curtains, robes. Not present in the current site — added to match your list.

### 07 · Décor & Accessories
Replaces the old "Table linen" tile with a broader décor bucket. Cushions, throws, table linen and finishing pieces.

## Decisions we need from you

1. **Wallpapers** — you didn't include this in your list. Options:
   - **(A)** Drop wallpapers from the site entirely
   - **(B)** Keep it as an eighth top-level category
   - **(C)** Fold it into **07 · Décor & Accessories** as a sub-item
   - *Our recommendation: (C) — keeps focus on windows/soft furnishings without losing an existing product line.*

2. **Sub-category listings inside Curtains & Windows** — do you want each sub-category (sheers, blackouts, blinds, etc.) to have its own scrollable section on the Collections page, or a single "Curtains & Windows" section with the sub-items listed as pills?

3. **"Complete home consultation"** as a service tile — currently offered in the enquiry-form dropdown only. Do you want it promoted to a top-level tile alongside the seven product categories?

4. **Brand list** — the homepage names ten brands (D'Décor, Marshalls, Asian Paints, Sleepwell, Kurlon, Duroflex, Peps, Bhadohi Weavers, Mirzapur Cooperatives, Bhuj Handloom). Please confirm which you're currently authorised to sell / actively stock. We'll strip any that don't apply.

5. **Category imagery** — every category tile currently uses a labelled placeholder. Please supply one clean, well-lit showroom or install photo per category (7 photos). Notes for each are captured in the `ph__note` label inside each tile.

1. Rebuild **collections.html** with these seven sections (in this order), each with an ID that matches the homepage links (`#curtains`, `#sofa-fabrics`, `#mattresses`, `#carpets`, `#bedding`, `#bath`, `#decor`).
2. Update the footer on every page to reflect the same seven categories.
3. Update the About and Gallery pages so category references and image captions align.

We will **not** touch collections.html, about.html, gallery.html or contact.html until you sign off on this structure and the homepage.
