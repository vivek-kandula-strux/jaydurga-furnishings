Privacy policy

We are a furnishing shop, not a data company. This page explains, in plain language, the little we collect and the little we do with it.

What the enquiry form collects

When you fill in the enquiry form on this website, we ask for:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your area or locality (so we can plan a visit)
  • What furnishing you are interested in
  • Any notes you choose to add — room details, window counts, preferred visit times

That is all. We do not ask for your email through the form, and we do not run any account system.

How we use it

We use your phone number — by call or WhatsApp — only to answer your enquiry and to arrange measurements and installation. Nothing else. No newsletters, no promotional broadcasts, no reminders you did not ask for.

What we never do

Your details are never sold, rented, or shared with advertisers or data brokers. They stay inside the shop, with the team that answers the phone.

Asking us to delete your details

You can ask us to delete your details at any time. Email hello@jaydurga.in from any address, mention the phone number you enquired with, and we will remove what we hold.

Questions

For anything about privacy, write to hello@jaydurga.in — or call +91 733 733 2652 during showroom hours.
