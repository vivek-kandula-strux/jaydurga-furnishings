Choosing

Firmness and size, without the guesswork.

Firmness is a shoulder-and-hip question, and your body answers it better than a spec sheet. That is why the beds are on the floor to try — and why the consultation is free.

  1. Tell us how you sleep

    Side, back, stomach, or all three by morning. It narrows eleven beds down to the two or three worth your time.

  2. Lie down on three

    A few minutes each, in your usual sleeping position. Take your time — nobody is counting.

  3. Check shoulders and hips

    If they sink too far, go firmer; if they press back, go softer. Your back will settle the argument.

  4. Size the bed, not the room

    Measure the frame before you visit — or ask about a free home measurement. Standard sizes are stocked; older and non-standard beds can be made to order.
