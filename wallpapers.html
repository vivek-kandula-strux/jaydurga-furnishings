One wall

A different room, without the civil work.

Wallpaper is the smallest civil work you can do to a house, and the one that returns the most. No breaking, no plaster dust, no week of painters — one wall of paper and the room reads new.

We keep Asian Paints Nilaya and Marshalls — India and UK — on the floor in Ameerpet, and bring in imported prints from Italy and Germany by special order. Textured and embossed papers, kids' room ranges, and the quiet solids that make a bedroom feel finished.

The process

How a wallpaper job runs.

  1. Site survey

    We visit your home, measure the walls, and check the surface and the light. The visit and the consultation are free.

  2. Choose the paper

    Sit with the catalogues at the shop — Nilaya, Marshalls, or the imported books for special order. Bring a photo of the room; it helps.

  3. Surface prep

    The wall is checked and prepared so the paste holds and the seams sit flat.

  4. Installation

    Hung by our own team — Naresh has been hanging paper for us since 2003. Bubble-free, seams aligned, edges clean.

  5. The promise

    If it is not bubble-free, we hang it again. No argument.

Ranges

Most customers start with one wall — behind the bed, behind the TV, the first thing you see from the door. Textured and embossed papers add depth that paint cannot, and the kids' room ranges are built to be wiped down and lived with.

Questions

Asked across the counter, answered here.

Is the site survey really free?

Yes. We visit your home, measure the walls, look at the light and the damp, and tell you what the job needs — before you spend anything. The consultation is free too.

Who hangs the wallpaper?

Our own team, not a contractor. Naresh has been hanging paper for us since 2003. Bubble-free — and if it is not, we hang it again, no argument.

Which brands do you stock?

Asian Paints Nilaya and Marshalls (India and UK) on the floor, with imported prints from Italy and Germany available by special order. Textured, embossed and kids' room ranges included.

What if something goes wrong after installation?

Warranty and after-sales support run through the shop — if something is wrong with the paper or the hanging, you call us, not a call centre.
