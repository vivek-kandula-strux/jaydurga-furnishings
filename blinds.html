The range

Which blind for which window.

Roller blinds are the simplest answer — a clean panel that disappears when you do not need it, in blackout cloth for the bedroom or solar screen where you want the view without the glare. Roman blinds fold up in soft pleats, in cotton or cotton-linen, where a window wants something gentler than a slat.

Venetian blinds — wooden or faux-wood — tilt on a cord, so you control the light without lifting the blind at all. Vertical PVC runs across wide windows and sliding doors, and honeycomb (cellular) blinds trap a layer of air in their folds, which keeps a hot room cooler and a cool room quieter.

Many homes end up mixing: blinds where the light needs a dial, and curtains where the room wants softness. We do both, so the two can be planned together.

Motorised

Quiet motors, fitted by our own electrician.

Motorised blinds open and close on a remote — useful on tall windows, hard-to-reach corners, or anywhere the cord would dangle. The motor is installed by our own electrician, and the warranty runs through the shop: if anything goes wrong, you call us, not a call centre.
